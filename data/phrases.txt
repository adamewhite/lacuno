# A line may carry a category, shown to the player under the header as a clue
# to the kind of answer:
#
#     Idiom | Cold feet
#     People | Marie Curie
#     Cold feet                 <- no category given, shown as "Phrase"
#
# Each phrase becomes one puzzle: one rack per word, in order. The player is
# given every CONSONANT as a scarce tile and an unlimited supply of vowels, and
# must reconstruct the phrase.
#
# What makes a phrase work here:
#   - recognisable enough that the skeleton is enough to trigger recall
#   - 2-5 words; more than that is a lot of racks on a phone
#   - not so long that the consonant pool becomes unwieldy (~20 tiles is a lot)
#
# Curate deliberately. This list is the game's content — the generator can no
# longer manufacture difficulty, so quality here is quality of the puzzle.
